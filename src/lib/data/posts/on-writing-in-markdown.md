# Why I Write Everything in Markdown

I keep almost everything in **markdown** — meeting notes, design docs, reading
lists, and now these blog posts. The reasons are boring, which is exactly why
they hold up.

## It's just text

A markdown file is plain text. It opens in anything, diffs cleanly in git, and
will still open in fifty years. No proprietary format, no lock-in.

## It's portable

The same file that lives in my Obsidian vault renders here on the site without
modification. Frontmatter carries the metadata; the body carries the words.

## It gets out of the way

Markdown's syntax is light enough that I stop thinking about it. A `#` for a
heading, a `-` for a list, `**` for emphasis — muscle memory, not friction.

> The best tool is the one you forget you're using.

That's the whole pitch. Write in text, keep your metadata close, and let the
rendering be someone else's problem.
